The problem

MFU students who don't read Thai can't read the rental market

  • Housing is advertised in Thai Facebook groups: rent, deposit, phone and soi all buried in free text.
  • Translating by hand costs minutes per post — the good rooms are gone before you finish.
  • The details that decide a visit are exactly what machine translation mangles: deposit months, soi names, distance to campus.
  • Students arrive in year 1 with a dorm, then must find off-campus housing themselves.

A typical post

ห้องให้เช่า ซอยแม่จันใต้ 3
ค่าเช่า 6000 บาท/เดือน
ประกัน 1 เดือน
ห่างจากมหาวิทยาลัย 10 นาที
สนใจโทร 081-234-5678

What the student actually needs

฿6,000 / month · 1 month deposit · 10 min from MFU · 081-234-5678

Who needs this product

Primary user, and who we deliberately don't serve

Primary: MFU international students

  • Can't read Thai, must rent off-campus after year 1.
  • Hunting in Thai-language Facebook groups, often on a phone.
  • Needs to answer the landlord in Thai but can't write it.

Secondary

  • Exchange / short-stay students arriving mid-semester.
  • Thai friends helping a foreign classmate shortlist rooms.

Three moments, one product

1 · Find — paste a post, read rent, deposit, phone, soi and distance in English.
2 · Compare — save listings, search, shortlist with ★, export CSV.
3 · Reply — tick the questions that matter, get a Thai message to paste into Messenger.

Not our user (by design)

Landlords and agents, full-time Thai renters, and anyone wanting a booking or payment platform — those are different products with different requirements.

What's built

Features, on both surfaces

Translate & extract

Chunked th→en translation plus price, deposit, phone, location and availability pulled from free text.

Distance to MFU

Stated minutes, a detected map link, or the Fah Thai market landmark — never an empty cell.

Saved list + shortlist

Search, ★ filter, one-tap call, original post link, photo thumbnails, expandable details.

Photo tray

Add, paste or drag photos; downscaled to ≤1000 px, saved with the listing, downloadable later.

Ask the landlord

Five common questions turned into Thai and copied in one tap — the app never sends it.

Export, alerts, theme

CSV export for comparing, area + budget alerts, light/dark theme, autosaved drafts, PWA offline shell.

The last three are supporting features — we don't count them as extra Must FRs, so the scope stays honest. Same workflow in the Chrome extension (inside Facebook) and the web prototype (any device).

Scope discipline

What we chose not to build

  • Auto-searching / scraping Facebook groups — platform terms; the user pastes or right-clicks instead.
  • Booking, reserving, rental payments — needs a payment provider and landlord agreements.
  • Contacting landlords automatically — FR-4 AC-3: the student reviews and sends.
  • Scam / red-flag detection — an unverifiable claim we can't honour.
  • Legal or contract advice — outside our competence and a liability trap.
  • Shared database, accounts, login — needs a backend; privacy by design (NFR-5).
  • Screenshot OCR in this MVP — excluded by the SRS; the UI says "not in this build".
  • Guaranteeing price or availability accuracy — the landlord is the source of truth.

15 explicit exclusions in docs/M2-srs.pdf §6. Saying no in writing is why the five FRs actually shipped.

#ServicePurposeAuth / costStatus

Chrome APIs: 6 families across 27 call sites — storage.local (8), runtime, contextMenus, tabs, downloads, action.openPopup. Permissions: storage, contextMenus, activeTab, downloads, unlimitedStorage.

Not APIs: Google Maps links are detected by regex inside the post; Facebook is read from the DOM the user is already viewing — no Graph API, no token, no group scraping. The unused Nominatim permission is a known gap we list in the report instead of quietly leaving it.

AI usage

No generative model in the product

At runtime

  • Translation only, via MyMemory — a machine-translation service that receives text and returns text.
  • Extraction is rule-based (regex + Thai unit words), as the SRS requires. No LLM decides a price.
  • That makes answers deterministic and testable — which is why we can run 11 tests on it.

During development

  • Claude (27 Aug) — extension development: translation, extraction, storage, feature suggestions.
  • ChatGPT (27 Aug) — organising the SRS into FRs and NFRs.
  • Codebuff (9–12 Nov) — M3 deliverables: web prototype, report, diagrams, deck, README, link and asset verification.

Everything AI produced was re-read, syntax-checked and tested before it landed; where output didn't match the requirements we rewrote it (logged in M2 §8). Disclosure: docs/ai-use-statement.md. Known weakness: the AI log is per-tool prose, not per-prompt.

Honest limits & next

What isn't there yet

  • Screenshot OCR — excluded by the SRS; the UI says "not in this build".
  • Translation — free MyMemory tier, fair-use quota, no offline model.
  • Distance — the landlord's figure or a landmark estimate, not a routed walk.
  • Storage — browser only, no account, no sync between devices.
  • NFR-3 — the 30-second first-use target is still unmeasured.

Next
