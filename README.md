Live demo: [baanright-web-4.2.vercel.app](https://baanright-web43.vercel.app/)

An off-campus housing assistant for international students at Mae Fah Luang University. Search listings, translate Thai housing posts, and save places to review later.

BaanRight turns Thai Facebook housing posts into English listings you can act on: it translates the post, pulls out the price, deposit, phone and availability, checks the distance to MFU, and saves everything — photos included — in one click.

## Features

- **Translate & extract** — paste a Thai post, get an English translation plus structured details (price, deposit, location, phone, availability) in one click. Press `Ctrl + Enter` to run.
- **Save images** — right-click any photo on Facebook → *Save image to BaanRight*, or add files from your computer. Tick the photos you want and they're attached to the listing when you save it; download them all again from the Saved tab.
- **Saved listings** — searchable list with one-tap *Call landlord*, *View original post*, photo thumbnails, a ★ shortlist, and details on demand.
- **Ask the landlord** — tick the questions you want to ask and generate a Thai message to send.
- **Export CSV** — take your saved listings into a spreadsheet for comparing options.
- **Preferences** — keep your target area and max budget handy for faster comparisons.
- **Works where you read** — select text anywhere (right-click → *Translate with BaanRight*, or the floating button) and it lands in the popup ready to translate.
- **Modern UI** — card-based layout, skeleton loading, toasts, and automatic light/dark mode.

## Install (Developer mode)

1. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode**.
2. Click **Load unpacked** and select this folder.
3. Pin BaanRight to your toolbar — or just select a Thai post and right-click.
